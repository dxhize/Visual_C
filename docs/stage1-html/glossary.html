66 · Terminology and glossary #

In one sentence: Use this as a quick translation when you meet a technical word elsewhere. Return to its linked chapter for the full example.

Key terms #

TermPlain meaning
EvidenceSomething actually recorded or statically described, with a source and limits.
InferenceA conclusion drawn from evidence. It may be useful, but it should never be relabeled as a directly recorded fact.

Example. GDB recorded raw_bytes_hex=0a000000. 'Four bytes were read' describes evidence. 'The C initializer ran' would be an inference that those bytes alone do not support.

>

What not to assume: A word such as 'complete' always needs a scope: complete for which processes, time interval and kind of record?

The big picture #

The same numeric value can identify different things at different boundaries. Start with a type's owner and scope before joining two records.

Recording terms #

TermMeaning
CheckpointRetained source-level snapshot at one observed stop, not every executed instruction.
Stop IDDebugger stop counter, not a snapshot vector position.
Attempted instructionAn attempt to record/advance at a PC; retention and gaps have separate accounting.
Retained eventActual indexed record in the instruction-event array.
GapExplicit interval of execution not recorded as exact instructions.
Allocation lifetimeWrapper-derived, scoped identity; an address may be reused by another lifetime.

Evidence terms #

TermMeaning
Structural findingImported trace contradicts a supported invariant; not simply an incomplete run.
PartialSome observation scope is absent, capped or interrupted.
UnavailableEvidence does not establish the requested relationship.
CandidateCompatible evidence without an exact proof under the documented scope.
EvidenceRefCitation bound to the same canonical trace owner and original record position.
Source-site candidateShared source file/line from an independent process; no primary allocation ID.
